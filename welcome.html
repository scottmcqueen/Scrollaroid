Scrollaroid

Whole-page screenshots, with crop and redaction built in.

1

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Scrollaroid. The blue page icon will then stay in your toolbar.

2

Capture

Full page

Click the icon. Scrollaroid scrolls the page itself and stitches it into one image.

AltShiftS

Selected area

Drag a box over just the part you want. Drag past the bottom edge to keep going down the page.

AltShiftA

As evidence

Records the address, time and fingerprints so the image can be checked later. More below.

AltShiftE

All of these are also in the menu when you right-click the icon. To stop a long capture early, press Esc or click the icon again; you'll keep everything captured so far.

3

Edit and save

  • Redact B Drag a black box over anything private. Click a box to move or resize it; Delete removes it.
  • Find & redact F Finds email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, usernames or any text you type, and covers them all at once.
  • Crop C Drag a frame, adjust the handles, press Enter.
  • Download or Copy when you're done. Black boxes are burned into the saved image, so nothing underneath can be recovered.

Press ? in the editor any time for the full list of shortcuts.

4

Capturing as evidence

Evidence captures hide nothing on the page, fingerprint every screen with SHA-256 the moment it's taken, and record the address, time, browser and window size. In the editor, Evidence package downloads a zip with the untouched original, your redacted copy, the raw screens, a PDF report and a checksum list anyone can verify.

This documents how a screenshot was made; it doesn't make it admissible on its own. Rules differ by country, so check with whoever is handling the matter.

Private by design. Scrollaroid has no servers, accounts or tracking. Screenshots never leave your computer, and temporary captures are deleted after 24 hours.
